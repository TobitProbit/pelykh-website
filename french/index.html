Mode d'emploi

For anyone in the family picking this up. No account, no app, nothing to install.

  1. Fifteen minutes, most days

    Short and often beats long and rare. The streak counter above is there to nag you, gently.

  2. Say it out loud

    In every "Parler" or speaking mode, answer aloud before you reveal the model, then shadow the audio. Speaking is the point.

  3. Mistakes come back

    Each page keeps a review deck of what you missed. The red badge on a lesson shows how many cards are waiting.

  4. Use a good French voice

    Audio uses your browser's built-in voices. Chrome and Safari have the best French ones; on a phone, check that a French voice is installed.

Progress is saved in your own browser on your own device, so each person keeps their own streak. Nothing is sent anywhere.
